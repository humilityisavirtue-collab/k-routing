What it checks
19 WCAG 2.1 criteria
Alt text, color contrast, form labels, ARIA landmarks, heading structure, page language, skip-to-content links, link text, and page titles — each mapped to a specific WCAG 2.1 success criterion.

Anything it can't auto-fix — complex image descriptions, PDFs, third-party widgets — it flags plainly. No false confidence. You'll know exactly what's left.
